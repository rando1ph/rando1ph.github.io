Privacy

What Hermes does with data it may be given access to — in plain terms.

Last updated: September 2026

Hermes is a private personal AI agent, operated by the site owner. This policy describes what happens when you authorise Hermes to reach your Google account.

  • Hermes may access Google services only after you have explicitly authorised it through Google's sign-in flow.
  • Depending on the permissions you grant, this may include Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts. Only the permissions you approve are used.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the personal-agent functionality you request.
  • Google user data is not sold.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising.
  • Google user data is not shared with unrelated third parties.
  • Authentication credentials and tokens are used only for authorised access to Google APIs.
  • When necessary to perform a requested action, Google data may be processed by the infrastructure used to operate Hermes.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google account's permissions or security settings.

This policy may change if Hermes' functionality changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
