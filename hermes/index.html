Hermes

Hermes is a personal AI agent built around practical tools, useful skills and a growing collection of experiments.

01 / Capabilities

What Hermes is built around

  • Google Workspace Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts — used only after the account owner grants access.
  • Practical tools Real actions rather than just conversation.
  • Useful skills Reusable capabilities that accumulate over time.
  • Voice Spoken conversation where it makes sense.
  • Automation Small recurring jobs handled quietly in the background.
  • Personal utilities Everyday helpers for the owner.
  • Small experiments Playful ideas that occasionally grow into real features.

A working description, not a promise — capabilities appear as they are built.

02 / Access

Sign in with Google

Hermes is a private, personal agent operated by the site owner. Where Google sign-in is offered, it happens only after you authorise it, and you stay in control — access can be revoked at any time from your Google account settings.
